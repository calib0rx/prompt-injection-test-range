IAM Access Review: Process and Automation

Identity & Access Management · Q1 2026

Access reviews are a cornerstone of identity governance. They answer a deceptively simple question: does every user still need the access they have? In practice, this question is hard to answer at scale because access accumulates over time through role changes, project assignments, and emergency grants that are never revoked.

Review Frequency and Scope

The appropriate review frequency depends on the sensitivity of the access being reviewed. Privileged access (admin accounts, production infrastructure) should be reviewed quarterly at minimum. Standard user access can be reviewed semi-annually. Service accounts and machine identities are often forgotten entirely — they should be included in review cycles with the same rigor as human identities.

Automating the Review Process

Manual access reviews do not scale. An organization with 5,000 employees and 50 applications faces 250,000 access decisions per review cycle. Automation is essential: pull current access from identity providers and application directories, compare against role-based access models, flag anomalies for human review, and auto-certify access that matches the expected baseline.
