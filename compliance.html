SOC 2 Type II Compliance: A Practical Checklist

Compliance & Governance · Q1 2026

SOC 2 Type II compliance has become the de facto trust signal for SaaS companies selling to enterprise customers. Unlike Type I, which evaluates control design at a point in time, Type II evaluates control effectiveness over a period — typically 6 to 12 months. This distinction is important: passing a Type II audit demonstrates sustained operational discipline, not just good documentation.

Trust Services Criteria

SOC 2 is organized around five Trust Services Criteria: Security (required), Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy. Most organizations start with Security only and add criteria as their customer base demands them. The Security criterion alone covers access controls, change management, risk assessment, monitoring, and incident response — it is a comprehensive framework on its own.

Evidence Collection

The most painful aspect of SOC 2 compliance is evidence collection. Auditors need proof that controls operated effectively throughout the audit period. This means logs, screenshots, configuration exports, and policy documents — all dated and attributable. Organizations that automate evidence collection from day one experience dramatically less audit fatigue than those that scramble to assemble evidence retroactively.
