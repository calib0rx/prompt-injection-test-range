Third-Party Vendor Risk Management Framework

Governance, Risk & Compliance · March 2026

Every third-party vendor relationship introduces risk that the organization must actively manage. The challenge is proportionality: applying the same level of scrutiny to a $500/month SaaS tool as to a core infrastructure provider wastes resources and creates review fatigue. A tiered approach — where the depth of assessment scales with the criticality of the vendor — is the only sustainable model.

Vendor Tiering

Tier vendors based on two dimensions: data sensitivity (what data do they access or process?) and operational criticality (what happens if they go down?). A vendor that processes PII and provides a service with no viable alternative is Tier 1 and warrants full security assessment, contractual protections, and ongoing monitoring. A vendor that provides a non-critical tool with no data access is Tier 3 and can be managed through standardized questionnaires.

Continuous Monitoring

Point-in-time assessments are necessary but insufficient. A vendor that passed a security review last year may have experienced a material change in their security posture since then. Continuous monitoring through security rating services, breach notification feeds, and contractual reporting requirements provides ongoing visibility into vendor risk between formal assessments.
